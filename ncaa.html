METHODOLOGY

How to read this

Who's included: players in the 2025–26 NCAA data with at least 10 GP who have three or four observed NCAA seasons in the 2018–19 through 2025–26 dataset. They therefore enter 2026–27 as a 4th- or 5th-year NCAA player in this database.

Eligibility caveat: this is a data-defined 4th/5th-year cohort, not an official NCAA eligibility certification. NCAA transition rules can make individual eligibility differ.

Current-season percentiles: position-specific within this cohort. Lower-is-better Puck Loss Rate is inverted so higher percentile always means better.

Historical comparable profile: 11 standardized metrics are combined across up to three NCAA seasons. The most recent season receives 60% weight, the previous season 25%, and the third-most-recent 15%.

Professional comparables: only same-position historical NCAA players who subsequently appeared in the AHL, DEL, or ECHL are considered. Up to five are displayed. Players with fewer than eight points in 2025–26 or without a sufficiently close statistical match receive no forced comparable.
