METHODOLOGY

How to read the data

Minimum sample: Players must have at least 10 games played in the selected season.

Position-specific benchmarks: Forwards are compared with forwards and defensemen are compared with defensemen.

Per-game values: Counting metrics are shown as per-game rates so players with different games played can be compared more fairly.

Percentiles: Higher percentiles always indicate stronger results. Lower-is-better metrics such as puck losses are inverted before ranking.

Faceoffs: Faceoff Win % appears only when the player meets the minimum faceoff sample.

Data source: InStat / ECHL player-tracking data used for this project.
